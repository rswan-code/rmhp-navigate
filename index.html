Phase 3 · Now Active
Deep Engagement & Barrier Visibility
Sustained engagement is surfacing barriers that standard outreach doesn't identify — social isolation, economic instability, and health literacy gaps that prevent members from accessing care. This is the intelligence that drives everything downstream.
What this data tells us "Are we learning anything meaningful that can change outcomes?" Yes. 401 PRAPARE screenings completed — 74% of screened members have at least one SDoH need, averaging 3.5 per member. Income is the dominant barrier at 84%, reflecting economic stress as the primary driver compounding social isolation. Social & Community (67%) and Food (62%) follow as the second and third dominant barriers — reflecting a Colorado Medicaid population where economic and food insecurity compound the social isolation driving care avoidance. Loneliness at onboarding is trending upward (1% in March → 53% in June), confirming we are reaching increasingly isolated members as outreach expands.
